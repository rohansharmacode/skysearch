Privacy

This network is run by the rescue team. We only receive what you send on this page. Your phone gets a random code instead of your name. While you are connected, the rescue drones measure your phone's signal strength to estimate where you are. We do not record your phone's hardware address. Messages stay with the rescue team and are deleted after the operation.
