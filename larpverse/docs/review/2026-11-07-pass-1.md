**R1-001 · P0 · acoustic/**
- **Where:** acoustic pile assignment (`acoustic/dsp.py` / `run.py`, hypothesis tests + triangulation); log at t=122/379/467; dashboard Pile priority.
- **Seen:** B1, where the weak-voice victim V3 is, gets priority 8 "No sound assigned" in all 3 listens, and in all 5 listens of the `--piles 5` run. Its transcript ("…anyone there water…") is assigned to C3 (silent; bearing 333–344° from the A2/A3 drones) and to B3 ("So it's anyone there watching"). The `dsp` run even makes a "debris" clip for B1. The final line says "Open leads for ground teams: B3 61, C3 46, C2 33", which are three piles with no one in them.
- **Expected:** the weak voice is assigned to B1, or to no pile. A ground team is never sent only to empty piles while a voice pile shows as silent.
- **Fix:** assign each separated source by triangulating across all drones' bearings. Reject the assignment if the intersection lands more than about 6 m from the pile centre, then check whether the bearing is mirrored for d3. Owned by BL-3.

**R1-002 · P0 · acoustic/**
- **Where:** beamformer / separation; clips `2-ac-B2` and `3-ac-B3` in `dashboard/data/state.json`.
- **Seen:** the drones are sitting at A3 (excluded), yet A3's loud voice comes out of the B2 beam ("Help, can anyone hear me?"; the `dsp` run gives "Help, can anyone hear my magic talk?"). That transcript justifies the B2 dispatch. From B2, "My leg is cut." (A3 again) becomes a B3 knocking lead at 61. The ping detail for B2-R1 shows "Heard here: voice 'Help, can anyone hear me?'".
- **Expected:** sources at excluded or visited piles are nulled, and the dispatch rationale reflects the knocking actually heard at B2.
- **Fix:** add LCMV null constraints toward excluded piles' strongest sources. Drop a clip whose transcript matches a clip already attributed to an excluded pile. Log "suppressed cross-talk from A3".

**R1-003 · P1 · acoustic/**
- **Where:** `acoustic/agent.py` fallback scorer + whisper stage; pile row C2 "Dog saying 'My name is Trapp.'; human 0.90".
- **Seen:** whisper hallucinates speech on non-speech sounds and the keyword scorer believes it. The dog becomes human 0.90 (lead 33). Knocking "My leg is cut." scores human 0.90. Junk transcripts reach the UI ("track.", "Follow is any water.").
- **Expected:** a dog, knock or debris class caps `human` unless VAD and whisper confidence are high. Low-confidence transcripts show as "[unintelligible]".
- **Fix:** gate transcripts on VAD speech ratio, whisper `no_speech_prob` and `avg_logprob`. Cap `human` by YAMNet class when it disagrees.

**R1-004 · P1 · acoustic/**
- **Where:** clip fields `doa_agree` and `reliability` (bar labelled "direction" in the dashboard).

- **Expected:** agreement reflects the triangulation residual, and reliability drops for wide cones and for mismatches between class and transcript.
- **Fix:** compute `doa_agree` from the per-drone bearing residual to the assigned pile (say within 15°), and derive reliability from it.

**R1-005 · P0 · sim/**

**R1-006 · P0 · sim/**
- **Where:** `sim/localise.py:27` `depth_nom = 1.5`; `sim/coordinator.py:475`; log "depth ≈1.5 m".
- **Seen:** every ping in every run is "≈1.5 m below the surface", because `z_est` = surface − 1.5 is a constant. Truth depths are 1.64, 0.89 and 1.16 m. The dashboard, caveat and handoff all call it an estimate (see R1-016 and R1-038).

- **Fix:** estimate depth from the slant ranges (with xy known, depth² ≈ r² − horizontal² per scan, then take the median) with an uncertainty. Until then, emit `depth_source: "nominal"` and say "assumed 1.5 m" in the log.
