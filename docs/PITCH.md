# 5-minute pitch outline

1. **Hook (0:00–0:30).** "Last year X people went missing in UK forests and hills." Find a real statistic from Mountain Rescue England and Wales and cite it. Then: every warm deer looks like a person on a thermal camera.
2. **User and problem (0:30–1:00).** The search coordinator. One pilot per drone, flying lawnmower lines, buried in false alarms.
3. **Live demo (1:00–3:00).** Open mission control and let a judge press the buttons:
   - kill a drone, and the regions re-split
   - start a fire, and the paths reroute
   - move the person, and the swarm re-finds them
   Point out a decoy being ruled out on-board.
4. **How it works (3:00–3:45).** Walk through one diagram: sense, then the Bayesian map, then Voronoi regions, then inspect, then confirm or ask a human. Everything runs on the drone.
5. **Evidence (3:45–4:20).** Benchmark table: 40% faster to first find, and 17 fewer false alarms per mission. Simulation only.
6. **What it can't do (4:20–4:45).** Simulated sensors, no wind, and dense canopy is still hard. Next steps: YOLOv8n on Jetson, then a field test.
7. **Close (4:45–5:00).** Why it matters to the coordinator: fewer false calls, faster finds, fewer pilots.
